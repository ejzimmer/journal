Projects Shelf

Vitality carried by saturation, size and a second row. Small cards pair into stacks so the shelf packs flush, and subtasks feed out of the card on a sheet of paper instead of pushing the row apart.

At app scale · interactive

    Chevron feeds the paper out · emoji cycles ready → in progress → done · esc closes

    How the packing works

    • Geometry A big card is exactly two small cards plus one gap. --big-h: calc(var(--small-h) * 2 + var(--shelf-gap)). Everything else follows from that one line: any row of the shelf is either one big-card-height or one small-card-height, so rows come out flush instead of ragged.
    • Stacks Small cards are paired into a wrapper before they hit the shelf. The wrapper is a single flex item holding two cards in a column, so the browser sees one big-card-shaped thing. No grid, no masonry library — the flex-wrap shelf is untouched.
    • Pairing rule Walk the list, buffer the small ones, emit a stack every second one. Big cards pass straight through. A leftover odd one sits alone. The list order is preserved apart from a small card occasionally waiting for a partner.
    • The paper Absolutely positioned under the card, so it is out of flow. The shelf cannot reflow when you open something. The slot animates grid-template-rows: 0fr → 1fr while the sheet inside slides from translateY(-100%) — the two together read as feeding out of a slot rather than unfolding.
    • Small cards too A chevron fades in on hover. Otherwise a ready project with subtasks would have no way to open, since only living cards carry a meta row.
