The Media Bookcase

Everything below is live — click a book to open it, click the stamp at its foot to move it along, rename anything by clicking it, add a book, give one the author it's been missing. Every series gets a stretch of shelf with its name on a hand-written card; its books are published to one trim size and banded at head and tail. The cover colour is the author, whose full name is printed on every spine.

The change underneath

Nothing, in storage. Books stay in the tree they're in today — an author owns series, a series owns books, and a book's parent is its path: media/books/<authorId>/items/<seriesId>/items/<bookId>. Giving an existing book an author is therefore a move rather than a field write, and the storage layer already does moves: moveItemBetweenLists re-parents atomically and keeps the id. What's missing is only the control — no component in the tab offers one — and that's what the dialog below adds.

The status stamp is the same: it reads and writes the medium and isDone already on the item, so there's no new field and nothing to migrate. The one addition anywhere is an optional band colour on a series node, written only when you pick one.

How it behaves

Status is a stamp on the spine

The stamp at the foot of each spine cycles the way today's medium button does, with finished folded in: unread → 📖 → 🎧 → ✓. It's the same two fields the tab already stores — medium and isDone — read as one cycle, so a finished book can't show a stale medium.

Click anything to rename it

Titles, author names and series names are all EditableText. Clearing a title deletes the item, exactly as the book row does now.

Fill in the blanks later

Author and series are ordinary fields on a book — comboboxes prefilled with whatever it already has. Add what's missing, correct what's wrong; typing a name that doesn't exist yet creates it, and the book moves to that shelf.

One dialog for both

Clicking a book opens it in a dialog; the + beside Books or Games opens the same shape of dialog to add one. Escape or a click outside closes either.
